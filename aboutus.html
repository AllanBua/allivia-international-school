ABOUT ALLIVIA INTERNATIONAL SCHOOL

Our Background

Allivia International School was established with the goal of providing quality, affordable and holistic education to learners in Uganda. We are a private co-educational day and boarding school offering Nursery, Primary and Secondary sections.

The school provides a welcoming learning environment where students are encouraged to think critically, work collaboratively and develop strong moral values from early years through to secondary level.

🎯OUR MISSION

To provide quality and holistic education that develops academically excellent, creative, disciplined and responsible learners who can positively contribute to society.

💡 OUR VISION

To become a leading centre of excellence in education, producing confident, innovative and responsible leaders for tomorrow.

OUR CORE VALUES

INTEGRITY | DISCIPLINE | RESPECT | EXCELLENCE | TEAMWORK
